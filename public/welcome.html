What Happens Next

How You'll Help Save Lives

Here's exactly what happens when someone in Puducherry needs your blood type.

1
🆘
Patient Submits a Request
A family member enters the blood type, hospital, and phone on our website. One form — that's all they need to do at 2am in panic.
→
2
🤖
Our System Finds You
Within seconds, every eligible donor of the matching blood type is ranked by distance to the hospital. If you're eligible, you're on the list — automatically.
→
3
📱
You Get a WhatsApp
You receive a personal WhatsApp message: blood type needed, hospital name, patient's contact, and your exact distance. Nearest donors hear first.
→
4
🦸
You Decide — No Pressure
Reply YES to confirm you're going. Reply NO to decline — no explanation needed. We never judge. Every hero has limits, and we respect yours.
