How It Works

From Request to Donor
in Under 60 Seconds

1
0 seconds
Patient Submits
Open the site, enter blood type, hospital name, and phone number. Hit submit. That's the only step the patient or family needs to do.

Why Donate Blood

One Donation.
Three Lives Saved.

Your single blood donation separates into red cells, platelets, and plasma — each saving a different person. You are more powerful than you know.

"The most powerful thing you can do for a stranger is show up when they need you most. That is what every Rotary Blood Line donor does."

— Rotary Club of Legacy, Puducherry
💉
Safe and Quick — Donating takes 30–45 minutes and causes no lasting harm. Your body fully replenishes blood within 24–48 hours.
📱
You're Always in Control — We only WhatsApp you when someone near you urgently needs your exact blood type. You can decline any time. No pressure.
⏰
90-Day Rest Period — You're eligible to donate once every 90 days. We track this automatically and send a friendly reminder when you're ready again.
🏅
Recognised by Rotary — Every donor who responds is acknowledged by the Rotary Club of Legacy as a Puducherry Community Hero.
